Player instructions

Play fair. Play safe.

Please do

  • Arrive 10 minutes early so you get the full hour
  • Wear non-marking sports shoes or flat studs
  • Carry your own water bottle and a towel
  • Keep the team to the booked number of players
  • Report any injury or damage to the desk

Please don't

  • Wear metal spikes on the turf
  • Smoke, chew tobacco or bring alcohol
  • Climb the nets or hang on the poles
  • Use a hard leather ball unless approved
  • Play beyond your slot, the next team is waiting

Booking policy: slot is held once the advance is paid. Reschedule free up to 4 hours before your slot. Rain delays are rescheduled at no charge.

FAQ

Quick answers.

How do I book a slot?

Pick a date and hour above, fill your name and number, and send the request on WhatsApp. We confirm once the advance is received.

What is the advance and is it refundable?

The advance is Rs 300 per hour booked. It is adjusted in your final bill and can be moved to another date if you reschedule 4 hours before.

Can I book for football?

Yes. Choose Football 5-a-side or 7-a-side in the booking form. The same slots and rates apply.

Do you rent equipment?

Stumps, bats, balls, football and bibs are available at the desk. Ask when you book.

What if it rains?

The ground is fully netted but open to the sky. If rain stops play before you start, we reschedule at no charge.
